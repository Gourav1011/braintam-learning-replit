# Braintam Live Class – Final Architecture & Roles (Production Version)

## Core Philosophy

Teacher = Teaching

Mentor = Operations

Student = Learning

The live classroom should keep every role simple and focused. Teachers should never be overloaded with attendance management, and mentors should never control teaching functions.

---

# Video Architecture

## Teacher

* Teacher video remains ON throughout the class.
* Teacher can share:

  * PPT
  * PDF
  * Whiteboard
  * Video
  * Screen Share
* Teacher can switch between:

  * Presentation Mode
  * Demonstration Mode
  * Split View

---

## Mentors

* Camera OFF
* Microphone OFF
* Join as viewers only.

Mentors are moderators and student support staff.

---

## Students

* Camera OFF by default.
* Microphone OFF by default.
* Join as viewers only.

Students can only become publishers temporarily when invited on stage.

---

# Stage System

Teacher controls the stage.

Maximum stage participants:
2–5 students.

Flow:

Student raises hand
↓
Teacher approves
↓
Student receives invitation
↓
Camera and microphone enabled
↓
Student appears on stage
↓
60-second timer starts
↓
10-second warning
↓
Auto remove from stage
↓
Student returns to viewer mode

Teacher controls:

✅ Give Mic
✅ Extend Time (+30 sec)
✅ Extend Time (+60 sec)
✅ Mute Student
✅ Remove Student

Only the teacher can control the stage.

Mentors cannot bring students on stage.

---

# Teacher Panel

Teacher should NOT see:

❌ Live
❌ Backstage
❌ Absent

Reason:
Teacher should focus entirely on teaching.

## Suggested Students

When mentors suggest students, the teacher should NOT receive:

❌ Popup
❌ Notification
❌ Message
❌ Mentor name

Instead:

The student's name should automatically:

* Move to the top of the Present Student List
* Be highlighted using a different color

Example:

Present Students

🟣 Rahul Sharma
🟢 Priya Singh
🟢 Arjun Patel

Legend:

🟢 Present Student
🟣 Mentor Suggested Student
🔴 Student Currently On Stage

This allows the teacher to instantly notice recommended students without being distracted.

Teacher can:

🎤 Give Mic
or
Ignore and continue teaching.

Mentor taps the phone icon and calls the student directly.

---

## Group Chat

Mentor sees only their assigned group's chat.

Example:

Group 1 Chat

Rahul:
I didn't understand the question.

Mentor:
I'll explain after this example.

Mentors cannot see chats of other groups.

---

## Raise Hand Queue

Raise Hand appears only when the teacher enables it.

Students raise hand.

Mentor sees raised-hand requests only from their assigned group.

Example:

Group 1 Raise Hand Queue

✋ Rahul Sharma
✋ Priya Singh

Mentor action:

[Suggest]

When Suggest is clicked:

* Student moves to the top of the teacher's Present Student List.
* Student name changes color.
* No popup appears.
* No mentor name is shown.

Mentors cannot:

❌ Give Mic
❌ Bring students on stage
❌ Control stage timer
❌ Control teacher video

---

## Mentor Permissions

Mentor can:

✅ Watch class
✅ Group chat
✅ Call absent students
✅ Monitor attendance
✅ Suggest students for mic
✅ View raised-hand queue of assigned group only

Mentor cannot:

❌ Use camera
❌ Use microphone
❌ Control stage
❌ Control polls
❌ Control teacher video
❌ Give mic directly

## Raise Hand

Raise Hand button appears only when teacher enables it.

Flow:

Raise Hand
↓
Mentor sees request
↓
Mentor can suggest student
↓

Student joins stage

## Student Restrictions

Students cannot:

❌ See attendance panels
❌ See other groups
❌ See absent students
❌ See backstage students
❌ See mentor staff chat
❌ Turn on camera themselves
❌ Turn on microphone themselves

# Internal Teacher ↔ Mentor Chat

Add a completely separate tab:

Chat
Poll
Staff Chat

Staff Chat is private.

Students cannot see this tab.

Purpose:

Mentor → Teacher

Examples:

"Rahul's internet is poor."

"Three students are absent."

"Please repeat the last question."

Teacher → Mentor

"Call absent students."

"Send worksheet in group."

"Ask Group 2 to stay after class."

"Check Rahul's internet issue."

Purpose:

Teachers and mentors communicate privately during class without using WhatsApp or external apps.

# Final Braintam Live Class Philosophy

Teacher = Teaching

Mentor = Student Operations

Student = Learning

Teacher focuses only on:

* Teaching
* Presentation
* Student interaction
* Giving mic

Mentor focuses only on:

* Attendance
* Group chat
* Calling absent students
* Supporting students
* Suggesting students for stage

Student focuses only on:

* Watching class
* Participating in chat
* Raising hand
* Coming on stage only when invited

This architecture is designed for large-scale classes (500–1,000+ students per class) while keeping the interface simple, scalable, and easy to use.
