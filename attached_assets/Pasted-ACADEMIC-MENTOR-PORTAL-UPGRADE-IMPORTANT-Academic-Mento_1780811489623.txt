ACADEMIC MENTOR PORTAL UPGRADE

IMPORTANT:
Academic Mentor is a Student Success Manager.

Their job is:

* Student engagement
* Parent communication
* Follow-ups
* Academic monitoring
* Doubt support
* Student retention

Mentors must NOT edit academic data.

They can only view and support students.

====================================================

1. NEW TAB: TODAY'S TASKS
   ====================================================

Create a dedicated "Today's Tasks" tab.

Purpose:
Mentor should immediately know who needs attention today.

Sections:

A. Follow-Up Due Today

* Student Name
* Last Contact Date
* Next Follow-Up Date
* Last Remark
* Call Button
* Update Follow-Up Button

B. Contact Pending
Students not contacted recently.

Rules:

Green:
Contacted within last 3 days

Yellow:
No contact in 4-7 days

Red:
No contact in 7+ days

C. Homework Follow-Up Required

Show:

* Homework pending
* Homework not submitted
* Missing submissions

D. Attendance Concern

Show:

* Low attendance
* Consecutive absences
* Students missing classes

E. Test Performance Concern

Show:

* Low scores
* Failed tests
* Performance decline

====================================================
2. MENTOR CONTACT FREQUENCY TRACKING
====================================

Goal:

Every student should be contacted at least twice every week.

Create metrics:

* Last Contact Date
* Days Since Last Contact
* Contacts This Week
* Contacts This Month

Dashboard Widget:

Students Not Contacted:

* 3 Days
* 7 Days
* 14 Days

Mentor should never lose track of students.

========================
