====================================================
ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES
=====================

SUPER ADMIN

Can:
Everything.

ADMIN

Can:
Manage operations.
Manage staff.
Manage students.
Assign batches.
Manage permissions.

MANAGER

Can:
Manage students.
Manage mentors.
Assign batches.
View reports.

ASSISTANT MANAGER

Permissions configurable.

SALES MENTOR

Can:
View assigned students.
Call parents.
WhatsApp parents.
Add notes.
Follow-ups.

Cannot:
Assign batches.
Manage permissions.
Delete records.

ACADEMIC MENTOR

Can:
Attendance.
Homework.
Reports.
View assigned students.

Cannot:
Assign batches.
Manage staff.

TEACHER

Can:
Classes.
Attendance.
Homework.
Recordings.

Cannot:
Manage operations.
