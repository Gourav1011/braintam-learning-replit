LEAD ASSIGNMENT ENGINE
-----------------------------------------------------

When a new lead is created:

1. Read student grade.
2. Find assigned sales mentor for that grade.
3. Check mentor workload.
4. Assign automatically.
5. Create notification.

If Auto Balance is enabled:
Assign using Round Robin.
