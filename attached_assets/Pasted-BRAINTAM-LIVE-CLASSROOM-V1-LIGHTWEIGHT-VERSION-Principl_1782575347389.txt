BRAINTAM LIVE CLASSROOM V1 (LIGHTWEIGHT VERSION)

Principle:
Video must consume most resources.
Everything else should be lightweight and event-based.

---

## LEFT SIDE (80%)

Presentation Area

Support:

• Canva Public Presentation Link
OR
• PDF Upload

Teacher presents slides.

Annotation:
Simple pen and highlighter only.

No heavy whiteboard engine.
No infinite canvas.
No shapes library.
No stickers.
No animations from our side.

---

## RIGHT SIDE (20%)

Top:
Teacher Camera

Bottom:
Dynamic Panel

Only ONE thing visible at a time:

1. Group Chat
   OR
2. Poll
   OR
3. Leaderboard Popup

Never show all together.

---

## GROUP CHAT

Text only.

No:

❌ Images
❌ Files
❌ GIFs
❌ Emojis spam
❌ Voice notes

Messages:

"Good evening sir"
"I got option B"

Mentor can reply.

---

## POLLS

Teacher can:

1. Publish saved poll
2. Create quick poll

When poll launches:

Chat hides.

Student answers.

Poll disappears.

Chat returns.

---

## LEADERBOARD

Show only Top 3.

Based on:

Correct Answer
+
Fastest Response

Display:

5 seconds

Auto close.

No permanent leaderboard.

---

## RAISE HAND

Default:
Hidden

Teacher enables:

Open Questions

Student sees:

✋ Raise Hand

Student clicks once.

No text popup.
No audio requests.
No video requests.

Teacher and mentor see:

✋ Aryan
✋ Riya

Mentor solves via:

Group Chat
or
WhatsApp.

---

## PAYMENT BUTTON

Floating Button:

💳 Upgrade Course

Small button.

Bottom Right.

No popup animations.

No banners.

---

## RECORDINGS

Use:

Google Meet Recording
OR
Zoom Recording

Store only:

recording_url

Do not upload videos to BTL servers.

---

## SOCKET EVENTS

Only:

chatMessage
startPoll
submitPoll
showLeaderboard
raiseHand
toggleRaiseHand
attendanceHeartbeat

Tiny JSON messages only.

---

## DO NOT BUILD IN V1

❌ Student cameras
❌ Student microphones
❌ Participant video grid
❌ Real-time collaborative whiteboard
❌ File sharing
❌ Voice notes
❌ Emoji reactions
❌ Animated badges
❌ Screen sharing by students
❌ Custom video streaming
❌ Recording processing

---

## TARGET SCALE

10–20 simultaneous classes
20–50 students per class

This architecture should run comfortably because:

Video → Meet/Zoom servers
BTL → Chat + Polls + Attendance + Raise Hand only

Keep everything simple, modular and lightweight.
