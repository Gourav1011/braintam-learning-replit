BTL CRM – Ignite to Mastery Auto Enrollment Flow

Example:

Grade 3
├── Mentor A → 100 Leads
└── Mentor B → 100 Leads

Total Leads = 200

During the 5-day Ignite Demo:

Lead #145 pays using any method:

1. Payment during live class
2. Mentor-generated payment link
3. Partial payment uploaded and approved by Admin
4. Full payment uploaded and approved by Admin

The moment payment status becomes PAID or APPROVED:

System should automatically:

Step 1:
Find the lead's grade.
Example:
lead.grade = Grade 3

Step 2:
Find the active Mastery course.

Query:
course_type = 'mastery'
grade = 'Grade 3'
status = 'active'

Example Result:
Mastery Grade 3 (Academic Year 2026-27)

Step 3:
Create Mastery enrollment.

student_enrollment

* student_id
* course_id
* grade_id
* enrolled_at
* enrollment_source = 'ignite_conversion'
* status = 'active'

Step 4:
Mark Ignite lead.

lead_status = converted
converted_at = timestamp

Step 5:
Immediately grant access.

Student App:
My Courses
────────────────
Ignite Grade 3
✓ Completed

Mastery Grade 3
✓ Active
────────────────

The new Mastery course should appear instantly in the student's Courses section without admin intervention.

Important Rules:

1. No manual enrollment.
2. No admin selecting courses.
3. No admin moving students between systems.
4. Payment approval is the trigger.
5. Grade is the single source of truth.

Flow:

Lead
↓
Assigned to Grade 3 Mentor
↓
Attends 5-Day Ignite Demo
↓
Payment Success / Admin Approval
↓
System reads Grade = Grade 3
↓
Find Active Mastery Grade 3 Course
↓
Create Enrollment
↓
Mark Lead Converted
↓
Course instantly appears in Student App
