   (Blue Border)

* Calling locked

Disabled

   (Grey Border)

ATTENDANCE PROTECTION

Mentors cannot modify:

* Past attendance
* Future attendance
* Teacher records
* Live class schedule

Mentors can modify only:

* Today's attendance
* Calling status
* Follow-up remarks
