BTL CRM – MODERN ONE-SCREEN IGNITE PORTAL

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

* Everything important should fit on one desktop screen (1920x1080).
* Minimize vertical scrolling.
* Use drawers and modals instead of opening new pages.
* Clean SaaS design with lots of white space.
* Use Braintam Blue and Orange as accent colors.

AUTO GROUP ENGINE
=================

Groups are temporary.

Do not create permanent group pages.

Example:

1000 leads
10 mentors

Automatically:

Group 1 → Mentor A → 100
Group 2 → Mentor B → 100
...
Group 10 → Mentor J → 100

Store history only.
