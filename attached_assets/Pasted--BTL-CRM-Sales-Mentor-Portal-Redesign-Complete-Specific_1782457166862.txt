## BUSINESS CONTEXT

Braintam mentors work like:

* ODA Counselors
* Physics Wallah Counselors
* CuriousJr Admission Counselors

Their main job is:

1. Call parents
2. Add remarks
3. Schedule follow-ups
4. Send long-term payment links
5. Convert demo students into long-term students

Mentors are NOT managers.

Mentors are NOT admins.

Mentors do NOT need complicated dashboards.

Portal should be:

* lightweight
* mobile friendly
* modern
* extremely fast
* minimal clicks

Everything should feel like a calling CRM.
