MASTERY PAYMENTS, NOTIFICATIONS & MENTOR EXPERIENCE IMPROVEMENTS

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT break existing Ignite CRM, Mastery CRM, Payments, Leaderboards, or Notifications. Extend the current system only.

────────────────────────
PART 1 – PAYMENT FLOWS
────────────────────────

Support only two payment methods:

1. Full Razorpay Payment Link
2. Partial/Manual Payment Upload

---

## FLOW A – FULL PAYMENT LINK

Parent pays through the generated Razorpay payment link.

Flow:

Payment Success
↓
Razorpay Verification
↓
Student Status = Active
↓
Automatically assign purchased grade course
↓
Course instantly appears in Student App
↓
Mentor Notification
↓
Leaderboard Update
↓
Achievement Ticker

NO ADMIN APPROVAL REQUIRED.

NO PAYMENT VERIFICATION QUEUE.

NO MANUAL ACTION REQUIRED.

Example:

Grade 4 Payment Link
↓
Payment Success
↓
Assign Grade 4 Mastery Course
↓
Course appears in My Courses

The course appearing itself acts as confirmation.

No additional payment confirmation messages are needed.

---

## FLOW B – PARTIAL PAYMENT UPLOAD

Examples:

Cash
Bank Transfer
UTR
Screenshot
Third-party EMI Proof

Flow:

Mentor Uploads Payment
↓
Payment Status = Pending Verification
↓
Admin Reviews
↓
Approve or Reject

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT show any notifications for:

Pending Verification
Partial Payments
Payment Uploads
Duplicate Checks
Verification Checks
Incomplete Payments

All of this should remain completely silent.

---

## CONVERSION RULE

A successful payment is counted only when:

1. Full Payment Link succeeds

OR

2. Admin approval causes:

# Total Amount Paid

Course Fee

Only then:

Student Status = Active
Course Auto Assigned
Mentor gets +1 Successful Payment
Leaderboard Updates
Achievement Ticker Generated

Before this point:

No conversion
No leaderboard update
No notifications

PART 3 – MENTOR NOTIFICATIONS
────────────────────────

Remove duplicate notification systems.

Current system has:

Bell
Flag

If both show similar notifications:

Remove Flag entirely.

Keep only:

🔔 Notification Bell

Notification Bell should contain:

🔔 Sumit completed successful payment.

Examples:

🔔 Aryan Sharma completed successful payment.
🔔 Neha Gupta completed successful payment.
🔔 Rahul Singh completed successful payment.

Generate only after:

Full Payment Link Success

OR

Admin approval completes the total course fee.

No notifications for partial payments.

────────────────────────
PART 4 – ACHIEVEMENT TICKER
────────────────────────

Create a live achievement ticker.

Visible only to:

Ignite Mentors
Team Leads
Managers

Do NOT show to:

Admins
Students
Parents

Location:

Top of Dashboard
Above KPI Cards

Notification text:

🏆 Mentor Sumit got 1 successful payment.

Behavior:

Message enters from left
↓
Moves to right once
↓
Disappears completely
↓
Next queued message begins

Do not loop continuously.

Do not use a marquee.

Do not repeat infinitely.

If there are multiple successful payments:

Queue them individually.

Example:

🏆 Mentor Rahul got 1 successful payment.
↓
🏆 Mentor Priya got 1 successful payment.
↓
🏆 Mentor Sumit got 1 successful payment.

Even if one mentor receives multiple payments simultaneously:

Generate separate queued messages.

Example:

🏆 Mentor Sumit got 1 successful payment.
↓
🏆 Mentor Sumit got 1 successful payment.
↓
🏆 Mentor Sumit got 1 successful payment.

Do not group into:

"Mentor Sumit got 3 successful payments."

Each achievement should feel like an individual win.
