raintam CRM Architecture Restructure (Workspace-Based Navigation)

Use the attached reference image as the target design direction.

Goal

Restructure the entire CRM into 3 logical workspaces:

🚀 Ignite (Sales & Admissions)

Contains ONLY:

Dashboard
Leads
Demo Students
Demo Batches
Attendance Analytics
Conversion Center
Payments
Sales Mentors
Reports & Analytics

Everything related to:

Lead → Demo → Conversion → Payment

belongs here.

📚 Mastery (Academic Operations)

Contains ONLY:

Dashboard
Courses
Teachers
Enrollments
Live Classes
Homework
Attendance
Announcements

Everything related to:

Paid Student → Learning Journey

belongs here.

⚙ Administration

Contains:

Operations Center
Super Admin
Audit Logs
Staff Attendance
Banners
Settings

Company-level controls only.

Important Navigation Change

Current system feels like:

Sidebar
   ↓
Ignite CRM
      ↓
Another Sidebar

This creates nested navigation and wastes space.

Remove the secondary navigation panel completely.

Use a Workspace Switcher at the top of the sidebar:

🚀 Ignite CRM ▼
📚 Mastery ▼
🌐 Enterprise ▼

When a workspace is selected, show only its menu items.

Enterprise View (Super Admin)

Add a third workspace:

🌐 Enterprise

Visible only to Super Admin.

Shows:

Overall Overview
Ignite Analytics
Mastery Analytics
Revenue
Student Growth
Mentor Performance
Operations Center
Super Admin
Audit Logs

Enterprise acts as the company command center.

Academic Mentors:

Super Admin:

Ignite
Mastery
Enterprise

Can switch between all workspaces.
