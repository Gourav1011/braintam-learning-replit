# Braintam CRM - Ignite Grade Teams & Organization Structure Redesign

Replace the current "Manage Grade Assignments" dropdown system completely.

The existing system (1 grade → 1 mentor dropdown) is not scalable and must be redesigned for 200+ mentors and thousands of leads.

---

# Core Principle

Keep these two modules completely separate:

## Module 1

Ignite → Grade Teams

Purpose:

* Assign mentors to grades
* Multiple mentors per grade
* Lead distribution
* Lead transfer
* Grade-level reporting

---

## Module 2

Organization → Team Structure

Purpose:

* Manager hierarchy
* Assistant Manager hierarchy
* Mentor reporting structure
* Performance reporting
* Leaderboards

Managers and Assistant Managers must NOT be configured inside Grade Assignments.

A mentor's reporting manager can change without affecting grade assignments.
