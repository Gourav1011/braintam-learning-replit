=========================
OUR PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE
=========================

We believe the platform should have ONE classroom engine.

Keep separate business programs.

Every classroom should exist inside

live_classes

using a field like

classType

Examples:

ignite
mastery
revision
competition
ptm

demo_batches should become scheduling/planning only.

Students and teachers should always join through

live_classes.
