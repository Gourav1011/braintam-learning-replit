PART 11 – CALL CONNECTED LOGIC & PERSON PICKED UP

Current issue:

Mentors can directly select:

Father
Mother
Student
Guardian

even when the call never connected.

This creates inaccurate data because we do not know who picked the call if the call was not answered.

Implement the following workflow.

═══════════════════════════════════════
CALL FLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════

Step 1:

Mentor clicks:

Call Status

Options:

Pending
Call Connected
Busy
No Response
Switched Off
Wrong Number
Call Back Later

═══════════════════════════════════════
CALL CONNECTED LOGIC
═══════════════════════════════════════

ONLY IF:

Call Status = Call Connected
Call Status = call later 

show an additional field:

Who Picked the Call?

Options:

Father
Mother
Student
Guardian
Other

Default:

Not Selected

This field must remain hidden for every other call status.

Example:

Busy
↓
Do NOT show:
Who Picked

No Response
↓
Do NOT show:
Who Picked

Switched Off
↓
Do NOT show:
Who Picked

Wrong Number
↓
Do NOT show:
Who Picked

Only:

Call Connected
↓
Show:
Who Picked the Call?

═══════════════════════════════════════
ADDITIONAL FIELD
═══════════════════════════════════════

After:

Call Connected
↓
Who Picked

Allow mentor to select:

Interested
Not Interested
Need Callback
Demo Explained
Class Details Shared

These options should also remain hidden until:

Call Connected = Yes

═══════════════════════════════════════
HISTORY TRACKING
═══════════════════════════════════════

Store complete history.

Example:

24 Jun
Call Connected
Picked By: Mother
Interested

25 Jun
Busy

26 Jun
No Response

27 Jun
Call Connected
Picked By: Father
Need Callback

Nothing should ever be overwritten.

═══════════════════════════════════════
NON-ACTIVE LEAD LOGIC
═══════════════════════════════════════

Primary decision should now be based on:

Call Connected.

A lead becomes Non-Active on Day 3 only if:

No Call Connected
No Demo Attendance
No Homework Activity
No Meaningful Engagement

Meaning:

Busy
No Response
Switched Off
Wrong Number
Call Back Later

DO NOT count as engagement.

Only:

Call Connected

counts as meaningful contact.

═══════════════════════════════════════

Busy

Call Connected Count
Connected %

═══════════════════════════════════════
FINAL REQUIREMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════

✔ Add "Call Connected" status.
✔ Show "Who Picked the Call?" only after Call Connected.
✔ Hide person selection for all other statuses.
✔ Save complete call history.
✔ Make Call Connected the primary engagement indicator.
✔ Use Call Connected in Non-Active Lead calculation.
✔ Keep reports and exports updated with these metrics.
✔ Do not break existing mentor workflows.
